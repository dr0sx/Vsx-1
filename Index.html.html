Quick Start

  1. Hover the right edge of the screen to reveal the Edit button.
  2. Use Visibility tab to enable/disable any component.
  3. Use Pics tab to upload images or paste URLs.
  4. Use Style tab to change colors, weather size, and fonts.
  5. Press F11 for full-screen TV mode.

Disabling Components

  • Go to the Visibility tab and toggle any section off.
  • Disabled items are completely hidden — no blank space left behind.
  • Toggle back on at any time — content is preserved.
  • You can disable individual slide types independently.

Changing Colors

  • Go to the Style tab and pick a preset theme or use custom color pickers.
  • Changes apply instantly across the entire display.
  • Adjust weather widget size (Small / Medium / Large) and days shown.
  • Toggle humidity, wind, and rain chance pills on/off.

Tips

  • Use 1920×1080 landscape images for best TV quality.
  • Weather refreshes every 10 minutes automatically.
  • The Edit panel auto-hides — hover the right edge to reveal.
  • All changes are live immediately — no reload needed.
  • Best viewed in Chrome or Edge browser.
